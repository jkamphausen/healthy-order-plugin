Before you start

This extension is part of a research study. To link your participation to the study, we ask for three pieces of information: your user ID (assigned by the study team), your gender and your age range.

  • The data is stored only in this browser (extension storage). It is never sent anywhere.
  • The user ID is a random number from the study team. It is not linked to your Uber account, your browser or any other identifier.
  • You can view, change or delete the data at any time on this page. Uninstalling the extension also deletes it.
